# Florida Earth Observation Data

FloridaView is a **gateway, not a duplicate archive**. Large datasets remain with durable repositories or the agencies that steward them, while FloridaView organizes the links, context, tutorials, and interactive products that help users work with those data.

## Where FloridaView data lives

| Need | Home | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Website pages, tutorials, figures, small downloads | GitHub Pages | Versioned, transparent, easy to maintain |
| Interactive maps and web layers | ArcGIS Online | Designed for web mapping and exploration |
| Large downloadable teaching/research datasets | Zenodo or authoritative source | Durable archive, citation, versioning |
| State/federal source data | Original agency portal | Keeps provenance and updates with the steward |

## FloridaView LiDAR lab data

The LiDAR learning path is published now. The associated large teaching datasets will be cataloged here only after their source, rights, spatial reference, version, checksum, and durable download location are verified. Until then, the data packages remain **in preparation** rather than linking to uncertain copies.

## Florida data portals

### Florida Geographic Data Library (FGDL)
Statewide GIS discovery and download resources maintained by the GeoPlan Center, including an interactive explorer, current-data catalog, and selected ArcGIS Online layers.

[Explore FGDL](https://fgdl.org/explore-data/)

### Florida Department of Environmental Protection (FDEP) Geospatial Open Data
Open environmental and program GIS layers, maps, applications, and StoryMaps from the Florida Department of Environmental Protection.

[Explore FDEP Geospatial Open Data](https://geodata.dep.state.fl.us/)

### South Florida Water Management District — DBHYDRO
Historical and current hydrologic, meteorologic, hydrogeologic, and water-quality observations for the South Florida Water Management District.

[Explore DBHYDRO](https://www.sfwmd.gov/science-data/dbhydro)

### St. Johns River Water Management District GIS
Geospatial data and tools including basemaps, imagery, land use, hydrologic information, and other district resources.

[Explore SJRWMD GIS](https://www.sjrwmd.com/data/gis/)

### Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission GIS
Downloadable and interactive GIS resources supporting wildlife, aquatic resources, conservation, and related research.

[Explore FWC GIS](https://myfwc.com/research/gis/)

### Florida Natural Areas Inventory (FNAI)
Conservation, natural-community, Florida Forever, and other ecological GIS resources.

[Explore FNAI GIS data](https://www.fnai.org/publications/gis-data)

## National Earth observation sources

- [USGS The National Map](https://apps.nationalmap.gov/downloader/) — elevation, LiDAR, hydrography, boundaries, and related national datasets.
- [NOAA Digital Coast](https://coast.noaa.gov/digitalcoast/) — coastal elevation, imagery, land cover, and coastal decision-support resources.
- [NASA Earthdata](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/) — NASA Earth observation datasets, services, and tools.
