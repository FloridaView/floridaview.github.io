# External Training Resources

Use these resources to extend the FloridaView curriculum beyond the LiDAR learning path. Links are maintained as pointers to the original publishers so learners reach the most current version.

## AmericaView

AmericaView provides national Earth observation education and outreach resources and an open CKAN resource portal covering tutorials, courses, publications, factsheets, and other materials.

- [AmericaView](https://americaview.org/)
- [AmericaView Resource Portal](https://ckan.americaview.org/)

## TNView

TNView publishes remote sensing and GeoAI learning resources, including cloud-native geospatial workflows and hands-on GeoAI tutorials.

- [TNView](https://tnview.org/)

## WV View

West Virginia View publishes free and open geospatial courses, training materials, software resources, and research outputs.

- [WV View](https://www.wvview.org/)

## NASA ARSET

NASA's Applied Remote Sensing Training program offers beginner through advanced training on NASA Earth data, web portals, tools, and applications. NASA moved new ARSET training announcements to Earthdata beginning in 2025.

- [NASA Earthdata ARSET](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/projects/arset)
- [NASA Applied Sciences training archive](https://appliedsciences.nasa.gov/get-involved/training)
