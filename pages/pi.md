# FloridaView Principal Investigator

## Caiyun Zhang, Ph.D.

**Professor and Director, Center for GIScience**  
**Department of Geosciences, Florida Atlantic University**

Prof. Caiyun Zhang leads FloridaView at Florida Atlantic University. Her research and teaching span **LiDAR and hyperspectral remote sensing, coastal vulnerability and sea-level-rise applications, machine learning and spatial modeling, and object-based image analysis**. FloridaView translates that expertise into durable Earth observation education, accessible training materials, and connections to authoritative data resources for Florida.

### FloridaView leadership

- Scientific direction and program stewardship
- Curriculum leadership for FloridaView educational materials
- Integration of remote sensing education with Florida-relevant environmental and geospatial applications
- Coordination with the broader AmericaView network
